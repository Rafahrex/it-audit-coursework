# IT Audit Coursework

IT audit and risk management work from the **Systems Audit** course at Universidad
Fidélitas (2026), translated into English. Both companies are fictional cases created for
the course.

| Project | Type | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [BlueTech CR: IT management audit](bluetech-iot-audit/) | Team (4 auditors) | 22-risk IT risk matrix and a 16-procedure audit work program for an IoT water-quality monitoring platform. I wrote procedures 9–12 (cloud backups, OTA firmware updates, malware and phishing, business continuity). |
| [Corporación Umbrella: clinical lab risk assessment](umbrella-lab-risk-assessment/) | Individual | Asset valuation (CIA), 5 risks rated by impact and probability, and 21 preventive, detective and corrective controls, framed around ISO 15189 accreditation. |

**Frameworks and standards used:** ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, COBIT 2019, ISO 15189:2022,
Costa Rica's public-sector audit and internal control standards, and Law 8968 on personal
data protection.

**Skills:** risk identification and rating, control design, audit planning, translating
technical risks (secrets in repos, OTA updates, ransomware, backups) into audit procedures.
